About Us

Staffing Solutions,
Built on Trust

TalentIQ Staffing LLC is a veteran-owned staffing and recruitment company committed to connecting exceptional talent with outstanding employers across the United States. Built on the core values of integrity, discipline, accountability, and service, our company brings a mission-first approach to every hiring partnership.

Founded by a U.S. military veteran, we understand that success is built on trust, commitment, and delivering results. These principles shape everything we do—from identifying highly qualified candidates to helping businesses build high-performing teams that drive long-term success.

At TalentIQ Staffing LLC, we specialize in permanent placement solutions across a wide range of industries, helping organizations find professionals who not only possess the right skills but also align with their culture and long-term vision. Our recruitment process is driven by market intelligence, rigorous candidate screening, and a personalized approach that ensures every placement is a strategic fit.

Whether you're an employer seeking dependable talent or a professional looking for your next career opportunity, TalentIQ Staffing LLC is dedicated to providing a seamless, transparent, and efficient hiring experience.
