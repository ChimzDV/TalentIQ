Our Recruitment Process

How TalentIQ Staffing LLC delivers a seamless, transparent hiring experience:

Step 1: Consultation & Intake

We schedule an in-depth call to understand your organization's environment, skill specifications, budget, and culture.

Step 2: Candidate Sourcing & Vetting

Our specialists utilize proprietary databases, jobs networks, and direct headhunting techniques to find top candidates. We screen profiles and short-list only the best matches.

Step 3: Interviews & Selection

We arrange candidate interviews at your convenience. You gain access to our detailed assessments to help make the final decision.

Step 4: Integration & Success Audit

Once chosen, we handle contracts, onboarding compliance, and check in regularly with your management to confirm candidate performance and satisfaction.
